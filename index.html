Quick answers

Where do my songs save?
The Song Library is shared. Anything you add with “Add to Song Database” shows up for every leader using this site, on any device. If you ever see an orange offline banner, your songs are saving to that device only until it reconnects.
How do I get my slides into Google Slides?
Hit Send to Google Slides. It downloads the file, then walks you through dropping it into Google Drive. Drive converts it and it opens in Slides with everything intact.
What happens to songs with language in them?
Curse words are replaced with *** automatically, both when you paste lyrics and again when the song goes onto a slide, so nothing slips onto the screen at club. That covers songs other leaders saved to the shared library too. Mild words like "hell" are left alone by default so worship lyrics are not broken, and there is a checkbox under the lyrics box to clean those as well. The filter only catches words, so still read through a song yourself before you put it up.
Why do my lyrics split into odd chunks?
Lyrics are automatically cut into four-line slides, and font size shrinks to fit long lines. Put a blank line between verses and choruses when you paste and it will break exactly where you want.
Why does my game slide just say NAMES?
On purpose. The screen shows numbered slots for the kids coming up front, not the instructions, so nobody reads the punch line before you run the game. The how-to stays here in the app for you.
Do YouTube videos play inside the slideshow?
In PowerPoint and Keynote, yes. Click the picture during your slideshow and it plays right there. It needs an internet connection, and some school and church guest networks block YouTube, so test it on the room's computer before club. Google Slides is the exception: it cannot play video that came in from a PowerPoint file. The link is saved in that slide's speaker notes, so in Slides use Insert → Video, paste the link, and it plays inline. The caption under the video is also a plain link that opens YouTube in a browser anywhere else.
Does my club night save if I close the tab?
No. Your club night is built fresh each time, so download the PowerPoint before you close the page. The song database and your ministry choice do carry over.
Can I use this for WyldLife, Capernaum, or YoungLives?
Yes. Pick your ministry on the Plan Your Club page and the whole club flow changes to fit it.
